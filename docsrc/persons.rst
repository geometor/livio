======
People
======

.. rubric:: Authors, Geometers, Historians & Scientists in ``geometor/livio``

.. _person-mario-livio:

Mario Livio (b. 1945)
---------------------

Astrophysicist (Space Telescope Science Institute; Technion) and author of *The Golden Ratio: The Story of Phi, the World's Most Astonishing Number* (Broadway Books, 2002).

.. _person-euclid:

Euclid of Alexandria (fl. c. 300 BCE)
-------------------------------------

Author of the *Elements*, codifying Division in Extreme and Mean Ratio (``II.11``, ``VI.def.3``, ``VI.30``), the regular pentagon and decagon (``IV.10–11``), the infinitude of primes (``IX.20``), and the five Platonic solids (``Book XIII``).

.. _person-islamic-algebraists:

Al-Khwarizmi (c. 780–850) & Abu Kamil (c. 850–930)
--------------------------------------------------

Founders of algebraic golden-section analysis: al-Khwarizmi's *Kitāb al-Jabr* ``10x = (10 - x)^2`` problem mirrors Euclid ``II.11``, and Abu Kamil's treatise on the pentagon and decagon influenced Fibonacci's *Practica geometriae*.

.. _person-sanskrit-prosodists:

Virahāṅka, Gopāla (fl. 1133) & Hemacandra (1088–1173)
-----------------------------------------------------

Indian prosodists who formulated the exact recurrence :math:`M_n = M_{n-1} + M_{n-2}` to enumerate Sanskrit *mātrā-vṛtta* poetic meters prior to Fibonacci's *Liber abaci* (1202).

.. _person-renaissance-polyhedra:

Piero della Francesca (c. 1415–1492), Luca Pacioli (c. 1447–1517) & Johannes Kepler (1571–1630)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Renaissance architects of polyhedral :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\phi)` geometry: Piero's *Libellus de quinque corporibus regularibus* and five-circle packing, Pacioli's *De Divina Proportione* (1509, illustrated by Leonardo da Vinci), and Kepler's :math:`1:\sqrt{\phi}:\phi` right triangle (1597), Fibonacci ratio limit (1608/1611), and pentagonal *Harmonices Mundi* tilings (1619).

.. _person-historiographers:

Roger Herz-Fischler (b. 1940) & George Markowsky
------------------------------------------------

Primary critical scholars whose documentary histories and statistical tests dismantled modern myths surrounding the Great Pyramid, Parthenon, *Mona Lisa*, Seurat, Fechner's rectangles, and Duckworth's *Aeneid* ratios.

.. _person-modern-physics:

Roger Penrose (b. 1931), Dan Shechtman (b. 1941) & Divakar Viswanath
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Pioneers of modern aperiodic and stochastic :math:`\phi` mathematics: Penrose's fivefold kite-and-dart / golden-rhombus tilings, Shechtman's 1984 discovery of icosahedral quasicrystals, and Viswanath's 1999 random Fibonacci growth constant :math:`1.13198824\dots`.

.. _person-phi-architect:

phi ARCHITECT
-------------

Founder of GEOMETOR and curator of exact symbolic models and Digital Scriptorium editions.
