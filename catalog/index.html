Published by Broadway Books in 2002, Mario Livio’s The Golden Ratio occupies a uniquely valuable position in the GEOMETOR golden-ratio canon alongside geometor/huntley (1970), geometor/knott (1996), geometor/divine, and geometor/posamentier (2011). Whereas popular compendia frequently repeat 19th-century folklore from Adolf Zeising (1854) and Matila Ghyka (1927), Livio pairs genuine algebraic, geometric, and physical theorems—from Hippasus’s pentagram descent and Piero della Francesca’s polyhedra to Douady–Couder ferrofluid phyllotaxis, Penrose–Gummelt quasicrystals, Divakar Viswanath’s random Fibonacci constant, and Benford’s Law—with rigorous historiographic and statistical refutations grounded in Roger Herz-Fischler (1987/1998) and George Markowsky (1992).

Exact Symbolic Verifiers (geometor.livio.invariants)¶

Every constructive theorem and quantitative myth-audit residual in geometor/livio is verified in exact SymPy arithmetic (guarded by assert_no_floats):

  • Pentagram Infinite Descent (LIV-02.04, LIV-APP.02): \(d_1 = \phi s_1,\ d_2 = d_1 - s_1,\ s_2 = s_1 - d_2 \implies d_2 / s_2 = \phi\) and \(s_2 / s_1 = 1/\phi^2 = 2 - \phi\).

  • Old Babylonian Susa Pentagon (LIV-03.01): Exact unit-edge area \(\frac{5\phi}{4\sqrt{3-\phi}} = \frac{\sqrt{5(5+2\sqrt{5})}}{4}\) vs. rational heuristic \(R_{\text{Susa}} = 5/6 \implies A_{\text{Susa}} = 5/3\) (1;40 base 60).

  • Khufu Pyramid Seked vs. Herodotus Myth (LIV-03.03, LIV-APP.03): Egyptian \(5\frac{1}{2}\)-palm seked per \(7\)-palm royal cubit yields \(h/a = 14/11\), simultaneously producing slant ratio \(s/a = \sqrt{317}/11 \approx 1.61859\) and perimeter-to-height \(8a/h = 44/7 \approx 2\pi\) without irrational constants.

  • Platonic Solids in \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\) (LIV-04.01): Unit-edge dodecahedron surface \(\frac{15\phi}{\sqrt{3-\phi}}\), dodecahedron volume \(\frac{5\phi^3}{6-2\phi} = \frac{15+7\sqrt{5}}{4}\), icosahedron volume \(\frac{5\phi^2}{6}\), and concentric dual edge ratio \(\phi^2/\sqrt{5}\).

  • Al-Khwarizmi’s Golden Division (LIV-04.06): \(10x = (10 - x)^2 \implies x = 15 - 5\sqrt{5} = 10/\phi^2\).

  • Piero della Francesca’s Five-Circle Packing (LIV-06.01): Five congruent circles inscribed tangentially in a ring inside a unit circle have diameter ratio \(\frac{\sin 36^\circ}{1 + \sin 36^\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{3-\phi}}{2 + \sqrt{3-\phi}}\).

  • Kepler’s Triangle (LIV-06.05): Hypotenuse segments \(AD = \phi,\ DB = 1 \implies CD = \sqrt{\phi},\ CB = \phi,\ AC = \phi\sqrt{\phi},\ AB = \phi^2\).

  • Major-to-Whole Compression Identity (LIV-07.05): For \(0 < m < M\) with \(r = m/M\), \(\left|\frac{M}{M+m} - \frac{1}{\phi}\right| = \frac{|r - 1/\phi|}{\phi(1 + r)}\), shrinking arbitrary deviations toward \(1/\phi\) by an exact factor \(\phi(1 + m/M) > \phi\).

Great Pyramid of Khufu (Ch. 3)

Refuted Myth

John Taylor (1859) fabricated the Herodotus II.124 h^2 = face area passage (Herz-Fischler, Markowsky); \(5\frac{1}{2}\)-palm seked (\(h/a = 14/11\)) explains both \(\phi\) and \(\pi\) coincidences.

Parthenon & Greek Vases (Ch. 4)

Refuted Myth

Arbitrary bounding-box selection; actual facade width/height is \(2.25\) (or \(\approx 1.72\) with steps).

Nautilus pompilius, Horns, Falcon & M51 (Ch. 5)

Refuted (\(\phi\)-specificity)

Scale-invariant growth produces general equiangular logarithmic spirals (\(\ln(r/r_0) = k\theta\)), not specifically the Golden Spiral rate.

Piero della Francesca & Leonardo’s 60 Polyhedra (Ch. 6)

Confirmed Intentional

Exact pentagon/icosahedron/dodecahedron algebra in Piero’s Libellus (plagiarized by Pacioli) and Leonardo’s 60 vacuus/solidus plates (1496–1498).

Leonardo’s Vitruvian Man, Mona Lisa & St. Jerome (Chs. 6–7)

Refuted Myth

Drawn/painted prior to meeting Pacioli (1496); Vitruvian Man explicitly encodes Vitruvius’s rational fractions (\(1/10, 1/8, 1/4\)).

Dürer’s Solid in Melencolia I (Ch. 6)

Refuted Myth

Perspectival reconstruction (Lynch 1982; MacGillavry) proves an \(80^\circ\) truncated rhombohedron on a \(4 \times 4\) grid, not \(72^\circ\).

Seurat (Parade de cirque) & Mondrian (Ch. 7)

Refuted Myth

Seurat used simple rational divisions (\(3:2, 8:5\), Herz-Fischler 1980); Mondrian worked intuitively by eye to “destroy mathematical formulas” (Yve-Alain Bois).

Sérusier, Lipchitz/Gris, Severini, Dalí & Le Corbusier’s Modulor (Chs. 1, 7)

Confirmed Intentional

Documented primary grids, Kepler triangles (Arlequin 1918), Dalí’s dodecahedral Last Supper, and Le Corbusier’s post-1945 Red/Blue Modulor scales.

Fechner’s Golden Rectangle Preference (Ch. 7)

Refuted Myth

Stimulus-range bias (Godkewitsch 1974), unpublished null ellipse test, and flat preference across \(1.5\text{–}1.75\) in 48-rectangle arrays (McManus 1980; Markowsky 1992).

Mozart Sonatas & Vergil’s Aeneid (Ch. 7)

Refuted Myth

Algebraic compression artifact of \(M/(M+m) \in (0.5, 1)\) (Curchin & Herz-Fischler 1981; Putz 1995); direct \(m/M\) scatter is random.

Sanskrit Prosody (Virahāṅka, Gopāla, Hemacandra) (Ch. 7)

Confirmed Intentional

Exact \(M_n = M_{n-1} + M_{n-2}\) enumeration of short/long syllabic mātrā combinations centuries prior to Fibonacci (Singh 1985).

Douady–Couder Phyllotaxis & Icosahedral Quasicrystals (Chs. 5, 8)

Confirmed Physics

Repulsive magnetic droplets converge dynamically to \(137.5^\circ\); Al–Mn/Al–Cu–Fe quasicrystals exhibit tenfold diffraction and \(\phi\)-step STM terraces.
