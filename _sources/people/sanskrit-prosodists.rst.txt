Virahāṅka, Gopāla & Hemacandra
==============================

Indian prosodists who formulated the exact recurrence :math:`M_n = M_{n-1} + M_{n-2}` to enumerate Sanskrit *mātrā-vṛtta* poetic meters prior to Fibonacci's *Liber abaci* (1202).
