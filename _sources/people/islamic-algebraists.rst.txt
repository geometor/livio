Al-Khwarizmi & Abu Kamil
========================

Founders of algebraic golden-section analysis: al-Khwarizmi's *Kitāb al-Jabr* ``10x = (10 - x)^2`` problem mirrors Euclid ``II.11``, and Abu Kamil's treatise on the pentagon and decagon influenced Fibonacci's *Practica geometriae*.
