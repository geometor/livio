Euclid of Alexandria

fl. c. 300 BCEDate: 300 BC

Author of the Elements, codifying Division in Extreme and Mean Ratio (II.11, VI.def.3, VI.30), the regular pentagon and decagon (IV.10–11), the infinitude of primes (IX.20), and the five Platonic solids (Book XIII).

Virahāṅka, Gopāla & Hemacandra

fl. 1133Date: 1133

Indian prosodists who formulated the exact recurrence M_n = M_{n-1} + M_{n-2} to enumerate Sanskrit mātrā-vṛtta poetic meters prior to Fibonacci’s Liber abaci (1202).
