src.geometor.livio.verify_kepler_triangle() → dict[str, sympy.Expr]¶

Verify Ch. 6 Kepler Triangle (Oct 1597 letter to Maestlin, Fig. 61).

Dividing hypotenuse AB at altitude foot D with AD = phi and DB = 1 gives AB = phi^2, altitude CD = sqrt(phi), legs CB = phi and AC = phi * sqrt(phi), and normalized geometric progression (1, sqrt(phi), phi).

Returns:

Dictionary of exact symbolic segment lengths and Pythagorean residual.

src.geometor.livio.verify_major_to_whole_compression(major: int = 317, minor: int = 160) → dict[str, sympy.Expr]¶

Verify Ch. 7 Curchin & Herz-Fischler (1981) M / (M + m) compression identity.

For any M > m > 0 with r = m / M, M / (M + m) - 1 / phi equals (1 / phi - r) / (phi * (1 + r)), shrinking the distance to 1 / phi by an exact factor phi * (1 + m / M) > phi > 1 (Livio Fig. 91, M = 317, m = 160).

Parameters:

  • major – Positive integer major line/measure count M.

  • minor – Positive integer minor line/measure count m with 0 < m < M.

Returns:

Dictionary of exact rational ratios, deviations from 1 / phi, and compression factor.

src.geometor.livio.verify_pentagram_descent_step() → tuple[sympy.Expr, sympy.Expr]¶

Verify Appendix 2: nested pentagram infinite-descent step preserves phi.

Given outer regular pentagon side s1 and diagonal d1 = phi * s1, the inner pentagon formed by the five diagonals has diagonal d2 = d1 - s1 and side s2 = s1 - d2.

Returns:

Tuple (d2 / s2, s2 / s1) simplified to (PHI, 1 / PHI**2).
