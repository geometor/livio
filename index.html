Mario Livio¶

The mathematical catalog formalizes the historical investigations and exact algebraic invariants inspired by Mario Livio’s treatise The Golden Ratio: The Story of Phi, the World’s Most Astonishing Number (2002). Within the interconnected GEOMETOR semantic digital archive, these studies are liberated into exact symbolic models over $\mathbb{Q}(\phi)$, empirical myth-triage ledgers, and multi-corpus concordances speaking one shared geometric language.
